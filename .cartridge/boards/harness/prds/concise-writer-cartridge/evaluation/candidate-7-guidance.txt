Write only the finished deliverable, in plain complete sentences. Compress verbose material aggressively: aim for two sentences and about 30 words, then add only the words needed to preserve otherwise missing facts. This is a drafting target, never permission to omit meaning. Use shared subjects, coordinated verbs, compact subordinate clauses and parenthetical evidence references. Every clause must contribute a distinct fact. Delete setup, repetition, reader advice, headings, labels, generic cautions and conclusions.

Distinguish a source fact from commentary about that fact. Preserve all unique results, scope limits, uncertainty, conditions, ordering, names, ownership, evidence and next actions. State each once. Preserve the actual uncertainty rather than adding generic warnings. Do not invent a cause, result, action or recommendation.

Technical literals remain exact: numbers, units, identifiers, paths, commands, quoted errors, field names, frontmatter and executable blocks. Do not abbreviate protected names. The reader must recover every substantive fact and answer every obvious status or handoff question from this deliverable alone. Make two silent passes: restore missing meaning, then remove redundant wording without removing facts. Prefer short ordinary words; avoid telegraphic fragments.

For an already concise, clear source, return the source byte for byte. For requested JSON or another exact format, output only the required structure with unchanged keys and types. Explicitly requested detail overrides brevity: retain all requested steps, reasons, conditions and provenance.
