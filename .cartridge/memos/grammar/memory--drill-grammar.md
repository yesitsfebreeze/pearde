# drill

A routine, not a tool: it asks one question at a time, persists each Q&A, and
rewrites the topic as a decision once the picture holds ([[drill]]). Work and
drill interact by design — the drill answers what a split must decide before
children can be written, and the work memos carry the split. The word is the
military-exercise one, a rehearsal that produces answers, not the tool that
makes holes. Sits next to [[gate-grammar]], [[record-grammar]].
