The tools of this repo, each with two pages: what it is, and what it scores.

**The scoring** works like @@health, but over tools instead of files: six
axes, weighted, one score of 1–10 per axis, a weighted total over 100, and a
ranking that names the worst first. A score is a **pointer, never a verdict**
— the good and the bad column say what pulls the tool down, and whether the
tool changes is the board's call.

| axis | measures | weight |
|---|---|---|
| `usability` | what it costs a person to run it: commands to remember, output to decode, setup to keep alive | 25 |
| `integration` | how it meets the rest — one reader of its format, one resolver of its paths, or a special case every caller carries | 20 |
| `sensibility` | whether its design still earns its shape — the concept count, the rules only its author holds, the parts you reread every time | 20 |
| `complexity` | files, branch points, concepts a newcomer must hold before the first useful run | 20 |
| `reliability` | how it fails — loud and named, or silent and plausible | 10 |
| `performance` | the wait it adds to the loop it sits in | 5 |

row. Per-tool detail lives on each tool's score page.

| obsidian | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 8 | 45 |
| view | 6 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 55 |

| health | 7 | 8 | 7 | 7 | 8 | 8 | 74 |
| board | 6 | 8 | 7 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 74 |
| workflows | 8 | 8 | 7 | 8 | 8 | 9 | 79 |

The two worst tools share one root cause: **state that lives outside the
board's own files** — Obsidian's register and Obsidian's plugin bundles, the
view's daemon and its port — so a broken one looks exactly like a working
one and only doctor tells the difference.
