Read @references/parts/personas.md — the signals table that picks one, the
three scopes, **What never switches it**, and why the choice is session state
in `PEARDE_AS` rather than a file on the board. The roster is
@references/personas/INDEX.md; the scope is `@@personas`.

A question aimed at one problem needs no switch: `pearde-persona-ask` changes
nothing. This skill is the switch itself.

Say the active persona and *why* the persona is the right reading for the work in front
of you. "engineer, because nothing here turns on a design call" is an answer;
the id alone is not.
