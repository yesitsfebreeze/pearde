# a-forcing-questions-gate-trades-comfort-for-evidence-before-a-plan-exists

The gate's job is to make a plan expensive to fake — questions are stage-routed so nobody answers six generic prompts, but every question that does run is pushed past comfort until it names a person, a workaround cost, or a surprise, and impatience is throttled to the two highest-value questions rather than honored outright. Evidence is the only thing that buys a full skip, which means the gate can't be argued out of, only satisfied.

Consequence: pearde PRD intake can apply the same rule — an analyst should refuse to draft a spec from a "just build it" premise until the PRD records who wants it and what they do without it today, and treat "skip the questions" as a request to answer fewer, not none.
