# No third-party vendor logo goes on this site; the client is named in words

Both vendors whose clients the site depicts require a written licence or a
partner agreement before their mark may appear in our marketing, and we hold
neither. Microsoft: a formal licence, with two narrow exceptions that an
integrations page does not meet. Slack: a written licence, with the App
Directory listing as the gate.

So the site names a client in words and never in its mark. That is not a
fallback we settled for — it is the only compliant option, and it happens to be
the one the design system would have chosen anyway: the recognition carries in
a caption a reader can read rather than in a 16px glyph they cannot.

Where a product shot depicts a client, the shot's own bar carries the name in
mono caps. The drawing below it reproduces the client's layout and ground so
the reader knows where they are, and reproduces none of its marks.

This holds until a partner agreement exists, at which point it is re-decided
per vendor and not in general.
