# The two-index loop is the discipline, not the tools

Pearde's scout answers jobs, not tools — and what made the 08-26/08-28 passes trustworthy transfers to any future measurement round:

1. **A job phrased as a choice.** If nothing was rejected, nothing was decided. The widget-API question is a finding precisely because it ended as a rejection with named survivors.
2. **At least two axes, and they must be allowed to disagree.** The ripgrep pick stands because four axes agree; the candle pick stands because they disagree loudly and the disagreement was read (`ort`'s install lead measures a different job — inference-only). One axis = `weak`, by rule.
3. **Numbers reproducible by route id.** Every cell re-runs via `route.sh <id> <query>`. A number no route reproduces is an opinion.
4. **Findings expire at six months**, and each carries its overturn condition in writing — what result would reverse the pick. A finding nothing could reverse was never a measurement.
5. **The queue is a queue, not a gap.** Un-measured jobs sit in Open with the axes to measure on.

This governs how knowledge notes get written here: a source states what a sweep measured with its route ids; a conclusion synthesizes only what ≥2 sources support.
