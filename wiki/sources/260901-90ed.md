# collect-report-routes-the-verdict

pearde collect --report reads the verdict word from the report head (first 40 lines, a Verdict: marker regex), maps it to the transition in-process through each command's own COMMANDS entry — so every gate the transition checks still runs. A missing or unknown word is refused with nothing written. Fixture-probed 2026-09-01 on the collect-reads-the-worker-s-report PRD: 14 checks green over a mktemp board — all six verdicts route, a red verify is still exit 1, SPECCED carries ## Scores onto the PRD and drafts a route when the slug is new.
