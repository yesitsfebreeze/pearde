# Sourced practitioners behind the skeptic persona

Seven practitioners, each verified against a real artefact, back one behaviour
of `@references/personas/skeptic.md`.

| practitioner | trait taken | source |
|---|---|---|
| Elisabeth Hendrickson | push zero, one, many and huge before believing it works | *Explore It!* (2013), appendix 2 "Test Heuristics Cheat Sheet" |
| Barton Miller | throw random malformed input at it before calling it reliable | "An Empirical Study of the Reliability of UNIX Utilities", *CACM* 33(12) (1990) |
| Ding Yuan | the untested error handler is where the catastrophe lives | "Simple Testing Can Prevent Most Critical Failures", OSDI 2014 |
| Joshua Bloch | close every resource on the exceptional path too | *Effective Java*, 3rd ed. (2018), item 9 |
| Steve Maguire | assert the assumption, or remove it | *Writing Solid Code* (1993), ch. 2 "Assert Yourself" |
| Titus Winters | price the change over the time it must be maintained | *Software Engineering at Google* (2020), ch. 1 |
| Andreas Zeller | shrink the failing input until only the failure remains | *Why Programs Fail* (2005), ch. 5 "Simplifying Problems" |

Dan Luu's "Files are hard" (2015) was read and rejected for the handle-close
behaviour — it covers ignored error codes, not resource lifetime. Bloch item 9
is the tighter match.
