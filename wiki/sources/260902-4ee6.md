# A harness on a full disk under-reports its count instead of erroring

workflow-improve's harness printed 50/71 where its baseline is 70/71, on a tree whose only change was seven prose files it does not read. No ENOSPC line appeared in its output. The volume had 255Mi free; the harness builds its fixtures with mktemp -d and a failed write inside a fixture reads as a failed assertion, not as an error.

Freeing space and re-running the identical tree printed 70/71. So a harness count below baseline is not evidence of a regression until df has been read — on a full disk every harness on this board that builds fixtures under mktemp -d degrades quietly, and the-next-line-runs, which copies the whole tracked tree, degrades first.
