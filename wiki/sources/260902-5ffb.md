# Slack's mark needs a written licence, and even the phrase 'integrated with Slack' is gated on an App Directory listing

Slack's brand terms: "Most uses require a specific written license, whether
through your separate agreement with us or a license specifically for this
purpose." The one clean path they name is the App Directory — "You are entitled
to say that your application is integrated with Slack or that you are a Slack
Platform Partner if you have an app in the Slack App Directory."

That is stricter than Microsoft in one direction and looser in another:

- Stricter on the WORD. Microsoft explicitly blesses plain-text referential use
  of "Teams"; Slack blesses no free-standing nominative use, only the two
  phrases above and only with a listing.
- Looser on the PICTURE. "Screenshots of the Slack software and website are
  permitted for instructive, educational or illustrative purposes" — unaltered
  except for resizing, no third-party content, no identifiable people. A
  stylised redrawing is not addressed either way.

Recolouring is forbidden for the LOGO — "Don't shuffle around the colors."
That is a rule about the mark, not about the UI: Slack itself renders a
third-party app's message attachment with a left bar in that app's own colour,
so an integration's own brand hue in that bar is Slack's documented behaviour
rather than a recolour of anything of Slack's.

Clear space for the horizontal logo is at least the width of one octothorpe
pill.
