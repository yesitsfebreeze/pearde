## 2. The divergence mechanism — `experiment_multi_family_probe`

One domain, round-robin full-batch SGD over `m` disjoint transforms:

```
d_model  families  lr        steps  first-div  held-out
    256         1  2.0e-3      762  None       0.3422
    256         2  2.0e-3      762  None       0.3414
    256         8  2.0e-3      762  Some(7)    NaN
    256         8  1.0e-4      762  None     169.4119
     96         1  3.0e-3      337  None       0.3479
     96         4  3.0e-3      337  None       0.3457
     96         8  3.0e-3      337  Some(12)   NaN
     32         8  5.0e-3      186  None       0.3977
     16         8  1.2e-2       47  Some(9)    NaN
```

At `m <= 4` it never diverges; at `m = 8` it diverges by step 12 at three of
the four widths. The rate is not the cause — dropping to 1e-4 keeps it finite
at a loss of 169, i.e. untrained. The cause is eight consecutive full steps,
each toward a different incompatible transform.

## 4. Disjoint, covering slices — `a0_arms_probe_slices_are_disjoint_and_covering`

At `PROBE_FAMILIES = 64`:

```
n=1   slice=64  overlapped=0 uncovered=0
n=4   slice=16  overlapped=0 uncovered=0
n=16  slice=4   overlapped=0 uncovered=0
n=64  slice=1   overlapped=0 uncovered=0
```

The predicate in `run_cell` today gives, at n=4, domain 0 = {0,1,4} and
domain 1 = {1,2,3,5} — overlapping — and at n=16 and n=64 every domain past
the eighth falls back to `i % 8` and shares.
