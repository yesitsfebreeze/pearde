# Sourced practitioners behind the designer persona

Seven practitioners, each verified against a real artefact, back one behaviour
of `@references/personas/designer.md`.

| practitioner | trait taken | source |
|---|---|---|
| Alan Cooper | trace the key path end to end before any screen | *About Face*, 3rd ed. (2007), key-path scenario |
| Steve Krug | eliminate instructions by making everything self-explanatory | *Don't Make Me Think, Revisited* (2014), ch. 5 "Instructions Must Die" |
| Steve Schoger | hierarchy is deliberate, or everything competes at once | *Refactoring UI* (2018), ch. 2 "Hierarchy Is Everything" |
| Jason Fried | cut the feature list in half, then in half again | *Getting Real* (2006), §5.1 "Half, Not Half-Assed" |
| Scott Hurff | design every state, not only the ideal one | "How to Fix a Bad User Interface" (2015), the UI Stack |
| Abby Covert | language is the material intent is built from | *How to Make Sense of Any Mess* (2014) |
| Jakob Nielsen | keep actions, terms and conventions consistent throughout | "10 Usability Heuristics" (1994), heuristic 4 |

Covert's chapter could not be confirmed — the citation is book-level on purpose.
Jesse James Garrett and Karen McGrane were checked and dropped: real, on topic,
but no line specific enough to a behaviour already better covered.
