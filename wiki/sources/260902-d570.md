# zirkle A0-pre's four per-arm learning rates are a transformer result and do not transfer to this tree's MLP

zirkle A0-pre closed the learning-rate confound over 28 runs on a two-block
transformer over `wiki.txt` at 150 steps. Its per-arm optima are 2e-3
(d_model 256), 3e-3 (96), 5e-3 (32) and 1.2e-2 (16) — a 6x rate span over a
16x width span, alpha = ln 6 / ln 16 = 0.646. Two of its own predictions are
refuted there: the 1/width law overpredicts arm 3 by 3.2x, and alpha is not
constant across the range (0.413 / 0.441 / 0.646). The honest statement is
the weak one: the optimum moves inversely with width, the exponent sits
between 0.4 and 0.65 over widths 16-256, and no single power law fits.

Measured in `infra/model` (a two-layer MLP over synthetic transformation
families, not a transformer over text), a nine-rate grid at 60 steps puts
the optima elsewhere: d=256 best at 1e-3, d=96 at 5e-3, and d=32 and d=16
BOTH still falling at 1.2e-2 — the edge of the grid. So importing zirkle's
four numbers as constants gives two arms a rate that is not their own, which
is the exact confound A0-pre existed to close, reintroduced by transplant.

A rate measured on one architecture over one corpus is a starting bracket
for another, never a constant. The transferable part is the direction and
the rough exponent, not the four values.
