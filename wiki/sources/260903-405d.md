# A registered claim split by a mark on the landing page must split where src splits its keys

The landing page's du-exemption reads whole text nodes, so a registered claim split by a <mark> is only exempt when the landing page splits it exactly where src/ splits it into its i18n keys.

src/i18n/address-form.test.ts exempts a du sentence when it is registered in docs/2-markenkern/claims-bibliothek.md (isRegistered, floor CLAIM_MIN = 20 chars) or when it is the German text of a key the site block already exempts. The landing block lifts sentences out of the HTML by splitting on tags, so a <mark> inside a heading yields two fragments.

Claim 26 is 'Manola spricht mit deinen Tools.' The site keys it int.title.a = 'Manola spricht mit' / int.title.b = 'deinen Tools.', and the joined .a/.b rule registers both. The landing page marked 'mit deinen Tools.' — a 17-char fragment, under the floor and not a key's text, so it failed the gate. Splitting the landing heading at the same point as src/ (mark carries 'deinen Tools.') makes it exempt through EXEMPT_TEXT and mirrors the site, which landing-mirrors-the-site wants anyway.

Rule of thumb: on landing/index.html, break a registered claim only where src/i18n/de/*.ts breaks it.
