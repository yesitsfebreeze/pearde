# nested-corners-need-concentric-border-radius

Nested rounded elements read as sloppy unless the outer radius equals the inner radius plus the padding between them — outerRadius = innerRadius + padding, never the same radius token copied onto both. A card at rounded-xl wrapping a button also at rounded-xl with p-2 padding is the single most common detail that makes an interface feel off. Fix it by raising the outer radius (rounded-2xl on the card wrapping a rounded-lg button) rather than matching tokens.
