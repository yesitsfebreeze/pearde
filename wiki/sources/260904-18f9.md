# retro-credits-teammates-with-anchored-praise-and-tracks-ai-commits-separately

Per-person write-ups require one praise line and one growth line each anchored to a real commit or metric ("every PR under 200 LOC" not "great work"), with the current user getting first-person deep-dive treatment and teammates getting two to three sentences. Co-Authored-By trailers credit a commit to the named human co-author but roll AI attribution into a separate "AI-assisted commits" count rather than treating the AI as a team member.
