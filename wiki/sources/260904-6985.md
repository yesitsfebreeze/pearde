# office-hours-impatience-buys-two-questions-not-a-skip

A user asking to skip the gate does not get skipped — the gate degrades to the two most critical remaining questions for that stage, and only escalates to a full pass-through on a second pushback. A full skip with zero questions is granted only when the founder arrives with a fully formed plan carrying real evidence (existing users, revenue, named customers), and even then the downstream premise-challenge and alternatives steps still run.
