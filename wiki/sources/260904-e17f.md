# office-hours-asks-only-the-forcing-questions-the-product-stage-needs

Six forcing questions exist (demand reality, status quo, desperate specificity, narrowest wedge, observation, future-fit) but a given idea is only run through the three to four that match its stage: pre-product gets demand/status-quo/specificity, an idea with paying customers gets wedge/observation/future-fit. Each question is pushed on until the answer names a specific person, behavior, or cost — a category ("healthcare enterprises") or a sentiment ("people say it's interesting") is refused as non-evidence.
